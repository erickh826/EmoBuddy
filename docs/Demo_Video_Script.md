## 英文版配音稿 (English)

**[00:00 - 00:10] Hook: The Problem**
*(Visual: Intro screen of EmoBuddy, calm and inviting)*
"Many children with ASD struggle to recognize and express emotions. Traditional apps are often passive screen-time. That’s why we built EmoBuddy—a phygital emotion-learning game."

**[00:10 - 00:30] Gameplay: Digital Exploration**
*(Visual: Clicking "Start Adventure", entering Level 1 "Happy Garden", moving the character)*
"EmoBuddy is a 2.5D exploration game designed with a low-stimulation, ASD-friendly environment. Children navigate simple grid maps to collect 'Emotion Shards'. Let's find the Happy Shard in the Happy Garden."

**[00:30 - 00:50] Core Feature: Real-World Camera Task**
*(Visual: Character reaches shard, camera modal opens, finds a red object, progress bar fills)*
"Once they find the shard, the magic happens. The game prompts a real-world task—like 'Find a red object in your room'. Using 100% on-device AI for colour and object detection, it’s completely private. No photos are ever taken or uploaded."

**[00:50 - 01:10] Parent Confirmation & Completion**
*(Visual: Parent unlock screen, long-press to confirm, Level Complete screen)*
"After the task, a caregiver confirms it with a 2-second long-press. This prevents accidental clicks and creates a shared moment of celebration. Complete all levels, and the child earns a custom certificate!"

**[01:10 - 01:20] Outro: Call to Action**
*(Visual: GitHub repo and live demo link)*
"EmoBuddy is open-source and ready to play. Visit our GitHub or try the live demo today. Let's make emotion learning a shared adventure!"
